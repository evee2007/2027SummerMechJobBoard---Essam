Resume tailoring setup

Your resume is already built in. Each job's Tailor button shows a tailored resume and cover letter right away. Add your phone and email once so they appear on the PDFs; they stay in this browser and are never published on the site.

Two ways to tailor. Free: press Copy prompt for Claude on a job and paste it into claude.ai. Automatic: add an Anthropic API key below and press Generate here (pay-per-use, roughly a few cents a job). The key is saved only in this browser; don't use this on a shared computer.

    How it's sorted: jobs with a deadline first (soonest at top), then Canadian roles, then oldest postings, since many close once they get enough applicants.

    "⚠ says no AI": shown when a job description you paste asks applicants not to use AI or to write in their own words, or when you flag a job yourself from its Tailor panel. For those, write the cover letter yourself; the Claude rewrite is turned off for them.

    Co-op roles: postings with "co-op" in the title usually need you enrolled in a school co-op program (TMU's starts in 3rd year), so they're hidden by default. Untick "Hide co-op roles" to see them; one that doesn't mention a school co-op requirement may still be worth applying to.

    2nd-year fit: "needs 3rd year+" means the posting asks for more school or co-op experience than a 2nd-year student has; those are hidden by default. "Stretch" means it leans toward upper years (often 12 to 16-month co-ops) but is worth a try with good projects. Most 4-month summer internships take 2nd years.

    Reading the tags: "check posting" means the link is a company careers search or the listing came from an aggregator, so confirm the role is still open and is a Summer 2027 term. "US citizens only (likely)" marks ITAR or defense roles that usually can't hire a Canadian citizen. Other US internships need a J-1 intern visa, which the employer sponsors; ask the recruiter early.

    Sources: company career sites, the SimplifyJobs Summer 2027 list, the thebargon and water473 GitHub mechanical trackers, and Indeed, Glassdoor and Workopolis listings, checked November 25, 2026. Many Ontario co-ops for May 2027 post through school job boards in January, so check TMU's co-op portal too.
